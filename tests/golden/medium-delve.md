### 👹 Room 1 — monster

A bone warden on its rounds holds the room (attack 7, health 18). The party must decide how to get past it. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 The caster opens with Dawnbreak, chosen precisely for the monster's holy weakness: spell damage ×1.5. ⚔️ A bone warden on its rounds is dead before the party closes: it never gets a round. 🧱 The party fights from behind stacked crates and barrels: 1 less damage per round. 🧱 The party fights from behind a fall of rubble: 1 less damage per round. 🏛️ Fighting from the aisles: 2 less damage per round on top of the cover. 🌟 The light soaks into a fall of rubble. Whatever was going to rise out of it stays put, and the working bites for 3. ❄️ Frost gets into a fall of rubble and cracks it apart: 2 damage in splinters of cold stone. 🧊 Frost sheets across an open pit until it will bear weight — a bridge to fight from, and a bad place to slip: 2 cover, 1 damage. 🛡️ The prayers on the inside of the buckler turn aside half of what the party set off. 🌐 There is room enough in the hall to fight spread out, well apart. A little less given and a little less taken, and only half of any blast reaches the party. 🦴 A bone warden on its rounds drops a pauldron of century bone: a trinket (+1 defense), now worn by Giordano Bruno.
