### 👹 Room 1 — monster

A bone warden on its rounds holds the room (attack 7, health 18). The party must decide how to get past it. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Melchior the Moth-Eaten made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 The caster opens with Radiant Lance, chosen precisely for the monster's holy weakness: spell damage ×1.5. ⚔️ A bone warden on its rounds is dead before the party closes: it never gets a round. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🗡️ The daggers land before the argument starts: nothing comes back in the first round. 🧱 The party fights from behind stacked crates and barrels: 1 less damage per round. 🔺 The party drives in as a wedge, everything committed forward. A fifth more damage dealt, a third more taken, and three of them swinging instead of two. 🦴 A bone warden on its rounds drops a pauldron of century bone: a trinket (+1 defense), now worn by Vex Threefingers.
