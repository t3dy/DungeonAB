⚗️ Room 1 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to dissolve the lock, or understand the mechanism and fix it — the Covetous refused to leave anything behind. The party chose to take one of the side passages.

🛤️ The side passage goes round, and round, and eventually through. Two more marches of oil burned than the direct road would have cost. +8 score.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A cinder imp wearing a snowball like armor holds the room (attack 11, health 20). The party must decide how to get past it. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to fall back, or sneak past — Cornelius Agrippa made the case: "Everything down here is carrying something. I collect." The party chose to stand and fight.

⚔️ The party kills a cinder imp wearing a snowball like armor in 2 rounds, taking 1 damage. 🛡️ Aegis of Ash goes up before the first blow: 2 less damage every round. 🛡️ Shields lock along the front rank and the party stops trying to win quickly. 🛡️ The party closes ranks: 2 less damage a round. 🎒 2 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🕯️ The lantern is burning low: oil for 1 more march.
