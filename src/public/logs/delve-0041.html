🕯️ Room 1 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to sing the third harmony, or link the three frequencies — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I did not survive the academy by standing in the open." The party chose to attune the resonances.

🎶 The resonances snap into attunement. The chamber sings with one voice, the party heals 2 apiece, and they carry the chord out with them: the next fight comes 1 damage a round softer. +22 score. 🎓 Not done alone, and better for it: +10 renown.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A chittering cloud of castle bats holds the room (attack 1, health 3). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "I've counted the exits. There are three. I love them all." The party chose to sneak past.

🗡️ The rogue leads the party past a chittering cloud of castle bats unseen. No damage taken; +15 score. 💡 Dancing Light revealed the watcher's position: +1 to the sneak roll.

🛡️ Margaret Cavendish takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind.

💰 Room 3 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to inspect it first, or leave it alone — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "There's a wire there. I noticed it while planning my retreat." The party chose to loot the treasure.

🦷 The chest is a mimic. It bites for 5 damage before the party kills it, recovering 23 gold.

✚ Margaret Cavendish is opened up badly enough that the delve will keep it: 10 is as whole as they get until town. The Reckless do not stop to bind anything, and more of it stays with them. The Devout tend what the dungeon opens: fewer blows leave a mark that stays.

💰 Room 4 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to search the room methodically, or divine what is hidden here — Kenelm Digby made the case: "I already know which part of it sells." The party chose to observe every detail.

👁️ Hidden in plain sight: an alcove holding coin and small valuables, overlooked by a hundred hurrying parties. +15 gold, +18 score. 🎓 Everything this asks for, the party happens to have brought: +25 renown.

🪐 Room 5 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or dissolve the lock — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Transmutation started as a hobby. It's a livelihood now." The party chose to take one of the side passages.

🛤️ The side passage goes round, and round, and eventually through. Two more marches of oil burned than the direct road would have cost. +8 score.

🪐 Room 6 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to read the maker's mark, or pick the chest that was opened before — Paracelsus made the case: "Gold in, gold out — that's the whole science." The party chose to appraise the three chests.

💰 Examined closely, the real chest is obvious — and so is how the other two were rigged, which is a lesson that keeps. +40 gold, +25 score, and the next snare is already understood. 🎓 Three disciplines on one problem, none of them guessing: +25 renown.

📚 Room 7 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to reconstruct the mosaic from memory, or recognize the mosaic — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "Even locks answer to providence. I just expedite." The party chose to imagine what the image should be.

✨ Your creative instinct pieces together something that was never there but might have been. A hidden cache reveals itself. +10 gold, +16 score. 🎓 Near enough to the discipline to try, not near enough to be paid for it: 10 renown short.
