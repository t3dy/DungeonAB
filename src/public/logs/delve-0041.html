🕯️ Room 1 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to sing the third harmony, or rest and heal — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I asked permission for this spell. Twice." The party chose to endure the discord.

🔊 The discord tears at the ears and nerves, and the party comes out of it well apart, each of them having walked away from the sound in a different direction. 3 damage, and no line to speak of.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A chittering cloud of castle bats holds the room (attack 1, health 3). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "What that thing drops will fund a semester of research." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 Frost Lance opens the fight; against a swarm the spell hits ×1.5. ⚔️ A chittering cloud of castle bats is dead before the party closes: it never gets a round. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🧱 The party fights from behind a row of squat pillars: 1 less damage per round. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 5 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ Margaret Cavendish walks in front, which is where the blows land first.

💰 Room 3 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to inspect it first, or leave it alone — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "Traps are just puzzles with stakes." The party chose to loot the treasure.

💰 The party loots the chest: 47 gold. 🍀 Also in the hoard: a dead adventurer's belt buckle (+1 defense), now worn by Margaret Cavendish.

💰 Room 4 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or search the room methodically — Kenelm Digby made the case: "I disarm faster when it's already ticking." The party chose to pry the sarcophagus open.

⚰️ The party pries the lid off a stone sarcophagus: 20 gold.

🪐 Room 5 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or dissolve the lock — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Transmutation started as a hobby. It's a livelihood now." The party chose to understand the mechanism and fix it.

🔧 The mechanism is understood and restored — it was a door-holder, and it shows the party how the builders meant to stand in this passage. The party takes that shape and keeps it. +22 score, and a shield wall into the next fight. 🎓 Done by people who do something near it: 10 renown less than a clean job.

🪐 Room 6 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to appraise the three chests, or read the maker's mark — Paracelsus made the case: "If it smokes, it works. If it screams, it works better." The party chose to take the heaviest chest.

💥 The heaviest chest was heavy because of what was packed around the lead. 5 damage, the party scattered across the floor, and only 10 gold in the wreckage — and it meets the next room spread out and unready.

✚ Margaret Cavendish takes a wound that will not close down here. Healing can bring them back to 10, no further, until town. The Reckless do not stop to bind anything, and more of it stays with them. The Devout tend what the dungeon opens: fewer blows leave a mark that stays.

📚 Room 7 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to reconstruct the mosaic from memory, or imagine what the image should be — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "I've counted the exits. There are three. I love them all." The party chose to strip the shelves.

⚠️ Room 9 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to disarm the trap, or search for a way around — Kenelm Digby made the case: "Even locks answer to providence. I just expedite." The party chose to push through and take the hit.

☠️ Margaret Cavendish falls. The party's wizard is dead; the 3 still standing march on.

⚠️ Room 10 — trap

They might have chosen to disarm the trap, or search for a way around — Paracelsus made the case: "Don't burn the carcass — half my income is in the glands." The party chose to push through and take the hit.
