👹 Room 1 — monster

A shriek of cinder bats holds the room (attack 6, health 11). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Michael Sendivogius made the case: "My shield works best with me behind it and everything else very far away." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 The caster opens with Hoarfrost, chosen precisely for the monster's frost weakness: spell damage ×1.5. ⚔️ The party kills a shriek of cinder bats in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 🗡️ The greatsword takes a whole rank of them at a stroke: 3 more damage a round. 🗡️ The daggers land before the argument starts: nothing comes back in the first round. 🎒 5 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ The order settles with Michael Sendivogius at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them. 🕳️ Johannes Trithemius finds a hidden door into the workshop wing. Behind it: cold furnaces and racked glassware. Its rooms join the route.

The party files through and leaves the room behind.
