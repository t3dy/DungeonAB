The transcript

🕯️ Room 1 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — Robert Fludd made the case: "Every door is a suggestion." The party chose to pry the sarcophagus open.

⚰️ The party pries the lid off a stone sarcophagus: 55 gold.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a hidden door into the barracks wing. Behind it: bunkrooms and a picked-over weapon rack. Its rooms join the route.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A frost wisp singed around the edges holds the room (attack 4, health 7). The party must decide how to get past it. It is ethereal: weapons do only 60% damage unless a cleric blesses the blades. It keeps clear of the torches: weak to fire (fire damage ×1.5). Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Robert Fludd made the case: "I disarm faster when it's already ticking." The party chose to sneak past.

🗡️ The rogue leads the party past a frost wisp singed around the edges unseen. No damage taken; +15 score. 💡 Dancing Light revealed the watcher's position: +1 to the sneak roll.

🛡️ The order settles with Tycho Brahe at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.

💎 Room 3 — vault

A hidden vault, stacked with treasure. Vaults always hold something beyond coin. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to open it with Knock, or inspect it first — Tycho Brahe made the case: "A fight you skip counts double." The party chose to loot the treasure.

💰 The party loots the chest: 164 gold. 🌿 Also in the hoard: 2 alchemy materials.

⚗️ Room 4 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or dissolve the lock — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Everything in this room fits in my satchel if I believe." The party chose to work the lab bench.

⚗️ The alchemist spends 1 material and applies fire coating to Tycho Brahe's weapon: +2 attack.

🕳️ The floor gives way — a hidden trapdoor. The party lands on the second floor, 2 rooms past where they were, taking 5 damage, and the rooms between go unlooted.

📚 Room 5 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to move on — Robert Fludd made the case: "Doors, guards, promises — all pickable." The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 1 spell.

🌿 Room 6 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather.

They might have chosen to move on — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Add nothing until you know what it does. Then add plenty." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 2 bundles of alchemy materials.

🌿 Room 7 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to move on, or harvest the spout — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Don't burn the carcass — half my income is in the glands." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 1 bundle of alchemy materials.

🐉 Room 8 — boss

The boss chamber. The Mad Pyromancer, delighted someone flammable came waits at its center (attack 14, health 31); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Kenelm Digby made the case: "Traps are just puzzles with stakes." The party chose to pry the sarcophagus open.
