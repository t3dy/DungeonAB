📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or move on — Paracelsus made the case: "Everything in this room fits in my satchel if I believe." The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 1 spell.

⬛ Room 2 — corridor

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible.

They might have chosen to accept the duel, or push past him — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "Even the dead tithe here. Especially the dead." The party chose to make it a team fight.

🎯 Four of them step up at once. He objects on principle, loses on arithmetic, and stands aside — and the party is already abreast when it reaches the next room. +20 score. 🎓 A second pair of hands that knows what it sees: +10 renown.

A fire vent meets the cavern ice: a scalding steam blast fills the room. The party must brace together or scatter.
