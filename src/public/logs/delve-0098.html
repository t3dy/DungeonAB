⚗️ Room 1 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to take one of the side passages, or move on — Robert Fludd made the case: "Doors, guards, promises — all pickable." The party chose to understand the mechanism and fix it.

🔧 The mechanism is understood and restored — it was a door-holder, and it shows the party how the builders meant to stand in this passage. The party takes that shape and keeps it. +22 score, and a shield wall into the next fight. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed below will open to this.

🧭 The party turns off into the burial wing — burial niches, most of them open. Its rooms join the route. 🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 6 rooms ahead and taking 3 damage.

💰 Room 2 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or search the room methodically — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to crack the crates open.

📦 The party cracks open stacked crates and barrels: 12 gold, 1 material.

🪐 Room 3 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to solve the progression, or read the dust — Kenelm Digby made the case: "Every door is a suggestion." The party chose to cross in order.

🎯 One at a time, weight where the weight has already been tested. Slow, sound, and the party is in single file at the far end. +18 score. 🎓 Somebody checks the work who is qualified to: +10 renown. 🗝️ Whoever built this had habits, and the party has just learned one of them.

🕯️ Room 4 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — Robert Fludd made the case: "Traps are just puzzles with stakes." The party chose to fill the waterskins.

⛲ The party fills its waterskins at a stone font of still water: 3 health healed.

📚 Room 5 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to reconstruct the mosaic from memory, or imagine what the image should be — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "The safety margin is where the good magic lives." The party chose to fill the waterskins.

⛲ The party fills its waterskins at a stone font of still water: 3 health healed.

🐉 Room 6 — boss

The boss chamber. The Glacier's Heart, half-melted and wholly furious waits at its center (attack 12, health 37); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It keeps clear of the torches: weak to fire (fire damage ×1.5). A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Kenelm Digby made the case: "Doors, guards, promises — all pickable." The party chose to drop the portcullis on it.
