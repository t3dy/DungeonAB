An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

🐉 Room 6 — boss

The boss chamber. The Glacier's Heart, half-melted and wholly furious waits at its center (attack 17, health 53); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It keeps clear of the torches: weak to fire (fire damage ×1.5). A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to put it onto the floor spikes.
