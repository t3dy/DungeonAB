🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to correct the orrery, or divine the unstable motion — Michael Sendivogius made the case: "Last one in buys the ale!" The party chose to repair the gears.

🔧 The gears are coaxed still and the walls stop. A stripped bronze pinion goes into the satchel: +8 gold, +20 score. 🎓 It is answered from three directions at once and stays answered: +25 renown.

💰 Room 2 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to pick the chest that was opened before, or take the heaviest chest — Simon Forman made the case: "I have a strong feeling we should be elsewhere. Call it prophecy." The party chose to loot the treasure.

🦷 The chest is a mimic. It bites for 5 damage before the party kills it, recovering 11 gold.

✚ Michael Sendivogius is opened up badly enough that the delve will keep it: 11 is as whole as they get until town. The Reckless do not stop to bind anything, and more of it stays with them.

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or move on — Tycho Brahe made the case: "Everything down here is carrying something. I collect." The party chose to strip the gold leaf.
