🌿 Room 1 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or search the room methodically — Tycho Brahe made the case: "My shield works best with me behind it and everything else very far away." The party chose to divine what is hidden here.

🔮 Divine sense finds what mortal eyes miss: a handful of coin scattered in crevices. +8 gold, +10 score. 🎓 It is answered from three directions at once and stays answered: +25 renown. 🗝️ The trick of the place is understood now. Somewhere below is a door that does not know that yet.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A pale hound with a red velvet collar holds the room (attack 8, health 15). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or sneak past — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "I've counted the exits. There are three. I love them all." The party chose to fall back.

💨 The party retreats, taking 2 damage on the way out. The room stays hostile; they will have to try it again.

🛡️ The order settles with Tycho Brahe at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.
