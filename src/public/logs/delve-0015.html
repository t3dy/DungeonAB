🌿 Room 1 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or search the room methodically — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to divine what is hidden here.

🔮 Divine sense finds what mortal eyes miss: a handful of coin scattered in crevices. +8 gold, +10 score. 🎓 Three disciplines on one problem, none of them guessing: +25 renown. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed below will open to this.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A pale hound with a red velvet collar holds the room (attack 10, health 17). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to put it down the crack in the floor.

🌑 The party works the monster to the edge and puts it into a crack across the floor: 15 damage on the way down. ⚔️ The party kills a pale hound with a red velvet collar in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 📌 They do not let it climb straight back out: 2 more damage from the room. ➖ The party spreads into a line, two forward and two behind. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 2 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ Tycho Brahe takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A chittering cloud of castle bats holds the room (attack 6, health 13). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to name the armour by its heraldry, or strip the insignia off it — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to speak with whatever wears the armour.

🪄 It answers at length, and mostly about a siege nobody else remembers. It asks only to be told the war is over — then falls in beside the party for the next stretch, shield up. +28 score, and a shield wall into the next fight. 🎓 Done by people who do something near it: 10 renown less than a clean job.

🐍 The venom carried from the last room acts: 2 damage taken on the march.

🪜 Room 4 — stairs

A stair cut into the rock, going down. Cold air comes up it.

They might have chosen to camp at the stairhead first — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕯️ Room 5 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to move on.

There is nothing to fight and nothing to take. The party keeps going.

🕳️ Johannes Trithemius finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands, skipping 2 rooms ahead and taking 3 damage.

⚠️ Room 6 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to disarm the trap, or push through and take the hit — the Reckless did not wait for a vote. The party chose to sift the rubble.

🪨 The party sifts a fall of rubble: 5 gold, 1 material.

🗝️ A guardroom door with a drop-bar — and the party has already read how this place was built. The barracks wing opens without a key and without a sound. 🚶 The party looks into the barracks wing (bunkrooms and a picked-over weapon rack) and keeps to the main route.

🐉 Room 7 — boss

The boss chamber. The Bride, who was here long before the Lord waits at its center (attack 20, health 56); killing it clears the dungeon. It is ethereal: weapons do only 60% damage unless a cleric blesses the blades. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to put it onto the floor spikes.
