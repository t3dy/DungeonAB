🌿 Room 1 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or divine what is hidden here — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Run first. The reaction can finish without us." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 1 bundle of alchemy materials.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A pale hound with a red velvet collar holds the room (attack 10, health 17). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to put it down the crack in the floor.

🌑 The party works the monster to the edge and puts it into a crack across the floor: 15 damage on the way down. ⚔️ The party kills a pale hound with a red velvet collar in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 📌 They do not let it climb straight back out: 2 more damage from the room. 🔺 The party drives in as a wedge, everything committed forward. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 2 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A chittering cloud of castle bats holds the room (attack 6, health 13). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to name the armour by its heraldry, or read its movement and walk around it — the Reckless did not wait for a vote. The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🐍 The venom carried from the last room acts: 2 damage taken on the march.

🪜 Room 4 — stairs

The floor ends at a stairwell. Whatever is below has been waiting longer.

They might have chosen to camp at the stairhead first — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕯️ Room 5 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to move on.

There is nothing to fight and nothing to take. The party keeps going.

🕳️ Johannes Trithemius finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

📚 Room 6 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to read the place itself, or reconstruct his rounds — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to scry for the map.

🔮 The map is under the flagstone he has been standing on for two centuries. He takes this news hard. +20 score, a march of oil saved.

⚗️ Room 7 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to cook a material down into lamp oil, or move on — Isabella Cortese made the case: "I keep my hazards bottled, thank you." The party chose to work the lab bench.
