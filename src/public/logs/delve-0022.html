The transcript

👹 Room 1 — monster

A thrall footman, polite and bloodless holds the room (attack 7, health 15). The party must decide how to get past it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber.

They might have chosen to accept the duel, or negotiate the terms — the Covetous counted what was in the room first. The party chose to make it a team fight.

🎯 Four of them step up at once. He objects on principle, loses on arithmetic, and stands aside — and the party is already abreast when it reaches the next room. +20 score. 🎓 A second pair of hands that knows what it sees: +10 renown. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed below will open to this.

🛡️ Michael Sendivogius walks in front, which is where the blows land first.

🕯️ Room 2 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — the Covetous counted what was in the room first. The party chose to bless the font and drink.

⛲ The cleric says the words over a stone font of still water and the party drinks: 12 health healed.

⚠️ Room 3 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A tripwire runs up the wall to a bell: an alarm trap. Little damage, but the next monster will be warned (+2 attack).

They might have chosen to search for a way around, or bypass it with a utility spell — the Covetous refused to leave anything behind. The party chose to push through and take the hit.

💥 The party pushes through the trap, taking 2 damage. 🔔 The alarm rings through the dungeon: the next monster will attack with +2.

🪜 Room 4 — stairs

The floor ends at a stairwell. Whatever is below has been waiting longer.

They might have chosen to rope down the shaft beside it, or camp at the stairhead first — the Covetous refused to leave anything behind. The party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕳️ The floor gives way — a hidden trapdoor. The party lands on the second floor, 3 rooms past where they were, taking 5 damage, and the rooms between go unlooted.

📚 Room 5 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to reconstruct the mosaic from memory, or imagine what the image should be — the Covetous counted what was in the room first. The party chose to break through the wall.

💥 The wall yields to force and the ceiling comes with it. 3 damage, a march of oil spent clearing the rubble, and what is left is a hole the party can only go through one at a time.

✚ 2 wounds on Michael Sendivogius, and the ceiling with them: 9, and no more.

🐉 Room 6 — boss

The boss chamber. The Vampire Lord, apologizing for the hour waits at its center (attack 18, health 49); killing it clears the dungeon. It is ethereal: weapons do only 60% damage unless a cleric blesses the blades. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Covetous refused to leave anything behind. The party chose to shove it into the brazier.

🔥 The party drives the monster into a brazier still burning: 16 fire damage. ⚔️ The party kills the Vampire Lord, apologizing for the hour in 3 rounds, taking 8 damage. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🔔 The alarm tripped earlier warned it: the monster attacks with +2 this fight. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🗡️ The daggers land before the argument starts: nothing comes back in the first round. 🎒 9 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🕯️ The lantern is burning low: oil for 1 more march.

✚ Giordano Bruno is opened up badly enough that the delve will keep it: 10 is as whole as they get until town. The Reckless do not stop to bind anything, and more of it stays with them.

☠️ Michael Sendivogius falls. The party's fighter is dead; the survivors march on.
