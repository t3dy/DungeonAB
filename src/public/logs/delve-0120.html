📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to study the shelves, or move on — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "Candles first. Then courage." The party chose to bless the font and drink.

⛲ The cleric says the words over a stone font of still water and the party drinks: 12 health healed.

🌋 Room 2 — disaster

The dungeon itself turns hostile. The party must brace together or scatter. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to pass a plan through the stone, or speak mind to mind — John Dee made the case: "Grace favors the well-prepared." The party chose to send something under the slab.

🪄 Something small and borrowed goes under the slab with a message tied to the idea of it, and comes back with an answer. Slower than thought, faster than digging. +22 score. 🎓 It is answered from three directions at once and stays answered: +25 renown.

🛡️ The order settles with Tycho Brahe at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.
