📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or study the shelves — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🗝️ Paracelsus pockets the archivist's key. Somewhere below there is a door it belongs to.
