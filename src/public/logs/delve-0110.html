The transcript

⚠️ Room 1 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A tripwire runs up the wall to a bell: an alarm trap. Little damage, but the next monster will be warned (+2 attack).

They might have chosen to push through and take the hit, or search for a way around — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Fundamentals of Sorcery, volume three, page ninety: this exact mistake." The party chose to bypass it with a utility spell.

There is nothing to fight and nothing to take. The party keeps going.

🛡️ Margaret Cavendish walks in front, which is where the blows land first.

💰 Room 2 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to appraise the three chests, or read the maker's mark — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "The commentaries disagree. I don't." The party chose to take the heaviest chest.

💥 The heaviest chest was heavy because of what was packed around the lead. 5 damage, the party scattered across the floor, and only 10 gold in the wreckage — and it meets the next room spread out and unready.

✚ Margaret Cavendish takes a wound that will not close down here. Healing can bring them back to 10, no further, until town.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A rattling skeleton patrol holds the room (attack 5, health 12). The party must decide how to get past it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 Fireball opens the fight, softening the monster before the first blow. ⚔️ A rattling skeleton patrol is dead before the party closes: it never gets a round. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🧱 The party fights from behind a fall of rubble: 1 less damage per round. 🎒 2 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🪐 Room 4 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to reconstruct the mosaic from memory, or imagine what the image should be — Giordano Bruno made the case: "Fascinating. Everyone stand behind me while I annotate." The party chose to break through the wall.

💥 The wall yields to force and the ceiling comes with it. 3 damage, a march of oil spent clearing the rubble, and what is left is a hole the party can only go through one at a time.

✚ 2 wounds on Margaret Cavendish, and the ceiling with them: 8, and no more.

🕯️ Room 5 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "There's a cheaper way to do this. There always is." The party chose to bless the font and drink.

⛲ The cleric says the words over a stone font of still water and the party drinks: 5 health healed.

👹 Room 6 — monster

A gelatinous horror holds the room (attack 4, health 15). The party must decide how to get past it. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to name the armour by its heraldry, or strip the insignia off it — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "The liturgy has a verse for this. Several, actually." The party chose to speak with whatever wears the armour.

🪄 It answers at length, and mostly about a siege nobody else remembers. It asks only to be told the war is over — then falls in beside the party for the next stretch, shield up. +28 score, and a shield wall into the next fight. 🎓 Everything this asks for, the party happens to have brought: +25 renown.

📚 Room 7 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to reconstruct his rounds, or scry for the map — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Fundamentals of Sorcery, volume three, page ninety: this exact mistake." The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🪐 Room 8 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to search the room methodically, or divine what is hidden here — Giordano Bruno made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to observe every detail.

👁️ Hidden in plain sight: an alcove holding coin and small valuables, overlooked by a hundred hurrying parties. +15 gold, +18 score. 🎓 Not done alone, and better for it: +10 renown.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault waits at its center (attack 10, health 28); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Fascinating. Everyone stand behind me while I annotate." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 Shelf-Found Working opens the fight, but the monster resists the element: spell damage ×0.5. ⚔️ The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault is dead before the party closes: it never gets a round. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🏛️ Fighting from the aisles: -2 less damage per round on top of the cover. 🎒 7 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🕯️ The wick is well down the oil. 2 more marches of light, then none.
