🕯️ Room 1 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to bring the fever down, or rest and heal — the Bold voted to meet it head-on. The party chose to push through and take the hit.

🤒 Robert Fludd is carried rather than treated, and the fever takes its price in full: 6 damage, and the party files through the next passage strung out around the litter.

✚ Robert Fludd is opened up badly enough that the delve will keep it: 9 is as whole as they get until town.

🪐 Room 2 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to negotiate with it, or investigate its claim — the Bold wanted it settled here. The party chose to answer it in its own tongue.

🌐 Answered in its own tongue, it stops being a monster in the passage and becomes someone with a complaint. It gives the party passage and a gift of its own reagents: +2 materials, +30 score.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A thrall footman, polite and bloodless holds the room (attack 5, health 12). The party must decide how to get past it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to accept the duel, or negotiate the terms — Giordano Bruno made the case: "Knowledge is treasure, but treasure is also treasure." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 The caster opens with Radiant Lance, chosen precisely for the monster's holy weakness: spell damage ×1.5. ⚔️ A thrall footman, polite and bloodless is dead before the party closes: it never gets a round. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🧱 The party fights from behind a row of squat pillars: 1 less damage per round. 🎒 5 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

💰 Room 4 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to divine what is hidden here, or open it with Knock — the Bold voted to meet it head-on. The party chose to observe every detail.

👁️ Hidden in plain sight: an alcove holding both materials and coin, overlooked by a hundred hurrying parties. +2 materials, +15 gold, +18 score.

🪜 Room 5 — stairs

A stair cut into the rock, going down. Cold air comes up it.

They might have chosen to camp at the stairhead first — the Bold wanted it settled here. The party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🌋 Room 6 — disaster

The candles go out: the castle itself attacks the party in the dark. The party must brace together or scatter.

They might have chosen to pass a plan through the stone, or speak mind to mind — the Bold saw no reason to be careful about it. The party chose to send something under the slab.

🪄 Something small and borrowed goes under the slab with a message tied to the idea of it, and comes back with an answer. Slower than thought, faster than digging. +22 score.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

📚 Room 7 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to read the place itself, or reconstruct his rounds — the Bold saw no reason to be careful about it. The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🪐 Room 8 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to endure the discord — the Bold saw no reason to be careful about it. The party chose to attune the resonances.

🎶 The resonances snap into attunement. The chamber sings with one voice, the party heals 2 apiece, and they carry the chord out with them: the next fight comes 1 damage a round softer. +22 score.

📚 Room 9 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells.

They might have chosen to study the shelves, or move on — Giordano Bruno made the case: "Range is a suggestion. Watch." The party chose to read the sealed texts.

🐉 Room 10 — boss

The boss chamber. The Bride, who was here long before the Lord waits at its center (attack 13, health 35); killing it clears the dungeon. It is ethereal: weapons do only 60% damage unless a cleric blesses the blades. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Giordano Bruno made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to open with a combat spell.
