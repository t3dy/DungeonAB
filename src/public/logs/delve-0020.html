🌿 Room 1 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or divine what is hidden here — the Cunning looked for the way that costs least. The party chose to put an edge back on at the anvil.

🔨 The party works a cold anvil: anvil-set edge on Robert Fludd's weapon (+3 attack).

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.
