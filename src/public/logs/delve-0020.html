The transcript

🌿 Room 1 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or search the room methodically — Paracelsus made the case: "The notes end mid-sentence. I intend to finish them." The party chose to put an edge back on at the anvil.

🔨 The party works a cold anvil: anvil-set edge on Robert Fludd's weapon (+3 attack).

📚 Room 2 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to study the shelves — Tycho Brahe made the case: "The body is a temple. Mine's a fortress." The party chose to move on.

The party moves on to the next room.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A gelatinous horror holds the room (attack 8, health 30). The party must decide how to get past it. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Robert Fludd made the case: "I already know which part of it sells." The party chose to sneak past.

🗡️ The rogue leads the party past a gelatinous horror unseen. No damage taken; +15 score. 👢 The Boots of the Quiet Step add +1.5 to the sneak roll. 💡 Dancing Light revealed the watcher's position: +1 to the sneak roll.

🛡️ The order settles with Tycho Brahe at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them. 🗝️ A sluice gate, wound shut. The rogue has it open in the time it takes to say so, and quietly. 🧭 The party turns off into the flooded wing — a floor that slopes down into standing water. Its rooms join the route.

🌋 Room 4 — disaster

The dungeon itself turns hostile. The party must brace together or scatter. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible.

They might have chosen to brace together, or scatter and regroup — Robert Fludd made the case: "My fingers itch. That means gold, or a rash." The party chose to sift the rubble.

🪨 The party sifts a fall of rubble: 5 gold, 1 material.

💰 Room 5 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to loot the treasure, or inspect it first — Paracelsus made the case: "Peer review can wait. The flask can't." The party chose to pry the sarcophagus open.

⚰️ The party pries the lid off a stone sarcophagus: 20 gold. ⚰️ The occupant objects: 4 damage before it is put back down.

🪜 Room 6 — stairs

The floor ends at a stairwell. Whatever is below has been waiting longer.

They might have chosen to camp at the stairhead first — Tycho Brahe made the case: "Last one in buys the ale!" The party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

👹 Room 7 — monster

A chittering rat swarm holds the room (attack 8, health 19). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. It keeps clear of the torches: weak to fire (fire damage ×1.5). Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to strip the insignia off it, or read its movement and walk around it — John Napier made the case: "Plan? I'm the plan." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 Chain Lightning opens the fight; against a swarm the spell hits ×1.5. ⚔️ A chittering rat swarm is dead before the party closes: it never gets a round. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 2 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🕯️ The lantern is burning low: oil for 1 more march.

🌋 Room 8 — disaster

They might have chosen to brace together, or scatter and regroup — Robert Fludd made the case: "Every door is a suggestion." The party chose to sift the rubble.

⚗️ Room 9 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to cook a material down into lamp oil, or work the lab bench — Paracelsus made the case: "The notes end mid-sentence. I intend to finish them." The party chose to harvest the spout.

🗿 The alchemist bottles what drips from a gargoyle spout, dripping: 1 material.

💡 Dancing Light kindles in the empty air and the party walks on seeing. The dark takes nothing.

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to strip the gold leaf, or move on — Tycho Brahe made the case: "The body is a temple. Mine's a fortress." The party chose to rest and heal.

The boss chamber. The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault waits at its center (attack 23, health 66); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5).
