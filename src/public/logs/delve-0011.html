⚗️ Room 1 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

⚗️ The lock dissolves. The mechanism doesn't open the door, but it doesn't need to now. +15 score. 🎓 A second pair of hands that knows what it sees: +10 renown. 🗝️ The trick of the place is understood now. Somewhere below is a door that does not know that yet.
