🌿 Room 1 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true.

They might have chosen to move on — Michael Maier made the case: "If it smokes, it works. If it screams, it works better." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 2 bundles of alchemy materials.

📚 Room 2 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to study the shelves, or move on — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to read the sealed texts.

🔏 The wizard reads the sealed texts: 2 spells learned, including a forbidden working. +50 score.

🧭 The party turns off into the flooded wing — a floor that slopes down into standing water. Its rooms join the route.
