💰 Room 1 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic.

They might have chosen to open it with Knock, or inspect it first — Robert Fludd made the case: "I've counted the exits. There are three. I love them all." The party chose to loot the treasure.

🦷 The chest is a mimic. It bites for 5 damage before the party kills it, recovering 11 gold.

✚ Cornelius Agrippa is opened up badly enough that the delve will keep it: 12 is as whole as they get until town.

🕯️ Room 2 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to strip the gold leaf, or move on — John Dee made the case: "I have a strong feeling we should be elsewhere. Call it prophecy." The party chose to rest and heal.

🕯️ The party rests at the shrine: 5 health healed per member.

🗝️ Robert Fludd pockets the archivist's key. Somewhere below there is a door it belongs to.

🪐 Room 3 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to attune the resonances, or link the three frequencies — John Dee made the case: "The god helps those who check for tripwires." The party chose to endure the discord.

🔊 The discord tears at the ears and nerves, and the party comes out of it well apart, each of them having walked away from the sound in a different direction. 3 damage, and no line to speak of.

🗝️ A reading-room door, locked since the last audit — and the party is carrying the archivist's key. The archive wing opens. 🚶 The party looks into the archive wing (shelving stacked to the ceiling, half of it collapsed) and keeps to the main route.

📚 Room 4 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or move on — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "I did not survive the academy by standing in the open." The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 1 spell.

🪜 Room 5 — stairs

Steps down, worn in the middle by traffic that stopped a long time ago.

They might have chosen to take the stair down — Robert Fludd made the case: "Every door is a suggestion." The party chose to camp at the stairhead first.

🏕️ The party makes camp at the stairhead and something climbs the stair into it: 6 healed each, 4 damage taken, and the second floor still to go. A night off their feet sets one of Cornelius Agrippa's wounds.

📚 Room 6 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to read the place itself, or question the ghost gently — Cornelius Agrippa made the case: "My shield works best with me behind it and everything else very far away." The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 1 spell.

🪐 Room 7 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to solve the progression, or ask which square is safe — John Dee made the case: "The god counsels prudence. Loudly. Through me. Right now." The party chose to read the dust.

👁️ Dust lies thick on the squares that never move. The party walks the dusty ones. +22 score. 🎓 Three disciplines on one problem, none of them guessing: +25 renown. 🗝️ Whoever built this had habits, and the party has just learned one of them.

🕯️ Room 8 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to push through and take the hit, or rest and heal — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "I know a spell for this. It's called leaving." The party chose to fill the waterskins.

⛲ The party fills its waterskins at a stone font of still water: 3 health healed.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Grand Errata, a book that reads back waits at its center (attack 5, health 14); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It keeps clear of the torches: weak to fire (fire damage ×1.5). A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to put it down the crack in the floor.

🌑 The party works the monster to the edge and puts it into a crack across the floor: 13 damage on the way down. ☠️ Even so, the Grand Errata, a book that reads back beats the party down. 🩸 Cornelius Agrippa takes the worst of it: 12 of the party's 12. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🔧 The party knows how to swing what the room left lying about: +5 to the opening. 📌 They do not let it climb straight back out: 2 more damage from the room. 💢 Sunder reminds the plate it was ore: it stops turning blows for the rest of the fight. 🎒 3 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ The order settles with Cornelius Agrippa at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.

☠️ Cornelius Agrippa falls. The party's fighter is dead; the 3 still standing march on.
