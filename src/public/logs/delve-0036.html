🌋 Room 1 — disaster

The candles go out: the castle itself attacks the party in the dark. The party must brace together or scatter.

They might have chosen to scatter and regroup, or sift the rubble — Tycho Brahe made the case: "My shield has a saint on it. She's watching. Form up." The party chose to brace together.

🌋 The party braces together and rides it out: 5 damage taken. 💚 Mending Word heals 10 as the dust settles.

🛡️ Tycho Brahe walks in front, which is where the blows land first. 🚶 The party looks into the barracks wing (bunkrooms and a picked-over weapon rack) and keeps to the main route.

🌿 Room 2 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather.

They might have chosen to dissolve the lock, or understand the mechanism and fix it — Paracelsus made the case: "As above, so below. Mind the fumes between." The party chose to experiment with the assembly.

🧪 Trial and error yields insight. The mechanism opens, and scattered materials reward the experiment. +2 materials, +20 score. 🎓 Nobody here is the person you would send. It shows, by 10 renown.

⚠️ Room 3 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to solve the progression, or read the dust — Kenelm Digby made the case: "I confess in advance. Saves time." The party chose to sift the rubble.

🪨 The party sifts a fall of rubble: 5 gold, 1 material.

⚗️ Room 4 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to work the lab bench, or move on — Michael Sendivogius made the case: "The body is a temple. Mine's a fortress." The party chose to harvest the spout.

🗿 The alchemist bottles what drips from a gargoyle spout, dripping: 1 material.

🕯️ The lantern is burning low: oil for 2 more marches.

🪜 Room 5 — stairs

A stair cut into the rock, going down. Cold air comes up it.

There was only one option: the party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕯️ The wick is well down the oil. 1 more march of light, then none.

📚 Room 6 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to read the place itself, or scry for the map — Paracelsus made the case: "The Work is a devotion. The explosions are incidental." The party chose to question the ghost gently.

🤝 Asked gently and given time, he remembers most of it — enough to save a march of oil, and to be a good deal calmer about the whole business. +22 score. 🎓 Not done alone, and better for it: +10 renown. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed below will open to this.

👁️ Eyes of the Mouse leads them through whole — none of the usual 3 damage.

👹 Room 7 — monster

Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Kenelm Digby made the case: "Even locks answer to providence. I just expedite." The party chose to open with a combat spell.
