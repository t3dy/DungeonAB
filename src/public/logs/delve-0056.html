⚗️ Room 1 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to dissolve the lock, or take one of the side passages — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to experiment with the assembly.

🧪 Trial and error yields insight. The mechanism opens, and scattered materials reward the experiment. +2 materials, +20 score. 🎓 Not done alone, and better for it: +10 renown. 🗝️ The trick of the place is understood now. Somewhere below is a door that does not know that yet.

🕳️ Nobody spots the door; the party works out where it has to be. The plan of the place demands a passage here, and here it is, into the flooded wing. Behind it: a floor that slopes down into standing water. Its rooms join the route.
