🌿 Room 1 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber.

They might have chosen to move on, or crack the crates open — Isabella Cortese made the case: "If it smokes, it works. If it screams, it works better." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 2 bundles of alchemy materials.

📚 Room 2 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to break through the wall, or move on — Paracelsus made the case: "Shake it and see." The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 1 spell.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A bog toad the size of a smokehouse holds the room (attack 10, health 26). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Robert Fludd made the case: "Traps are just puzzles with stakes." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 Cause Fear opens the fight, softening the monster before the first blow. ⚔️ The party kills a bog toad the size of a smokehouse in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🗡️ The daggers land before the argument starts: nothing comes back in the first round. 🎒 8 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ The order settles with Isabella Cortese at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.

🪜 Room 4 — stairs

Steps down, worn in the middle by traffic that stopped a long time ago.

They might have chosen to take the stair down — Kenelm Digby made the case: "I disarm faster when it's already ticking." The party chose to rope down the shaft beside it.

🪜 The party ropes down the shaft beside the stair and lands on the second floor. 🪢 The line goes down the shaft beside the stair: the party descends without burning a march of oil.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 3 rooms ahead and taking 2 damage.

🕯️ Room 5 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to strip the gold leaf, or move on — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Shake it and see." The party chose to rest and heal.
