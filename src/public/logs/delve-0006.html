The transcript

👹 Room 1 — monster

An obsidian golem with a slow fuse holds the room (attack 10, health 24). The party must decide how to get past it. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Kenelm Digby made the case: "The lock's a three-pin Herrengrave. The book was wrong about them. I'm not." The party chose to sneak past.

🗡️ The rogue leads the party past an obsidian golem with a slow fuse unseen. No damage taken; +15 score.

📚 Room 2 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to move on — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 2 spells.

🕯️ Room 3 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or move on — the Cunning looked for the way that costs least. The party chose to strip the gold leaf.

⛏️ The party strips 30 gold of leaf from the altar. The next disaster will hit harder for it.

🗝️ Cornelius Agrippa pockets the sexton's key. Somewhere below there is a door it belongs to.

🪐 Room 4 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to strip the insignia off it, or put the armour down — the Cunning had already worked out the odds. The party chose to read its movement and walk around it.

🎯 Eleven paces, a turn, eleven paces back. The party crosses in the gap and comes out the far side abreast and unhurried. +18 score.

🗝️ A barred grille, its bar on the far side — and the party is carrying the sexton's key. The burial wing opens. 🚶 The party looks into the burial wing (burial niches, most of them open) and keeps to the main route. 🕳️ Kenelm Digby finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 1 room ahead and taking 2 damage.

⚠️ Room 5 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. Scorch marks fan out from a seam in the floor: a fire trap. A frost spell can blunt it. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to disarm the trap, or push through and take the hit — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to bypass it with a utility spell.

The party crosses the room and takes the far door.

🌿 Room 6 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to gather the materials, or crack the crates open — the Cunning looked for the way that costs least. The party chose to move on.

There is nothing to fight and nothing to take. The party keeps going.

⚗️ Room 7 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or take one of the side passages — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to dissolve the lock.

⚗️ The lock dissolves. The mechanism doesn't open the door, but it doesn't need to now. +15 score.

💰 Room 8 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to inspect it first, or leave it alone — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to loot the treasure.

💰 The party loots the chest: 30 gold. 🍀 Also in the hoard: a whetstone of surprising opinion (+1 attack), now worn by Kenelm Digby.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Forge Tyrant, hammer still warm waits at its center (attack 20, health 48); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Cunning looked for the way that costs least. The party chose to fight from behind the pillars.

🏛️ The party backs into a row of squat pillars and makes the monster come down one aisle at a time: 4 damage as it closes. ⚔️ The party kills the Forge Tyrant, hammer still warm in 4 rounds, taking 18 damage. 🩸 Most of that lands on Cornelius Agrippa — 9 of the 18 taken. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🗡️ The daggers land before the argument starts: nothing comes back in the first round. 🛡️ Aegis of Ash goes up before the first blow: 2 less damage every round. 🎒 10 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

✚ 2 wounds on Cornelius Agrippa, and the ceiling with them: 10, and no more.
