The transcript

⚗️ Room 1 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or dissolve the lock — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Every explosion is a lesson. Class is in session." The party chose to take one of the side passages.

🛤️ The side passage goes round, and round, and eventually through. Two more marches of oil burned than the direct road would have cost. +8 score.

⚠️ Room 2 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to push through and take the hit, or search for a way around — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Range is a suggestion. Watch." The party chose to bypass it with a utility spell.

Nothing here needs doing. The party walks on.

🛡️ The order settles with Isabella Cortese at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.

📚 Room 3 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or study the shelves — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🪜 Room 4 — stairs

The floor ends at a stairwell. Whatever is below has been waiting longer.

There was only one option: the party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕳️ Isabella Cortese finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

🌋 Room 5 — disaster

A shelf of jars breaks over the party; what spills is corrosive and moving. The party must brace together or scatter.

They might have chosen to brace together, or scatter and regroup — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Add nothing until you know what it does. Then add plenty." The party chose to sift the rubble.

🪨 The party sifts a fall of rubble: 25 gold, 2 materials.

🕯️ The wick is well down the oil. 2 more marches of light, then none.

🌿 Room 6 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to gather the materials, or move on — Paracelsus made the case: "Measure twice, pour once." The party chose to harvest the spout.

🗿 The alchemist bottles what drips from a gargoyle spout, dripping: 3 materials.

🕯️ The lantern is burning low: oil for 1 more march.

🕯️ Room 7 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to bring the fever down, or diagnose and treat the fever — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Range is a suggestion. Watch." The party chose to push through and take the hit.

🤒 Isabella Cortese is carried rather than treated, and the fever takes its price in full: 6 damage, and the party files through the next passage strung out around the litter.

🕯️ The lantern dies with the party still under the hill. No more light to carry.

✚ That one leaves a mark on Isabella Cortese. Their ceiling drops to 11 for the rest of the delve.

👹 Room 8 — monster

An imp still angry about the jar holds the room (attack 10, health 19). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to speak with whatever wears the armour, or name the armour by its heraldry — John Dee made the case: "Grace favors the well-prepared." The party chose to shove it into the brazier.

🔥 The party drives the monster into a brazier still burning: 16 fire damage. ⚔️ The party kills an imp still angry about the jar in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🗡️ The greatsword takes a whole rank of them at a stroke: 3 more damage a round. 🔨 The Blessed Mace sanctifies the room between swings: whatever was stirring in the stone settles. ⏸️ The chamber is too tight to spread out: the party files up, one blade forward. 🎒 1 more preparation holds besides — the ledger keeps it.

🌑 The party gropes through the dark and pays for it: 2 damage to everyone. The Bold walk the dark like a road they know. It costs them less than it should. 🚶 The party looks into the barracks wing (bunkrooms and a picked-over weapon rack) and keeps to the main route.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Cauldron, which learned to want waits at its center (attack 25, health 70); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to fall back, or open with a combat spell — the Cunning had already worked out the odds. The party chose to stand and fight.

☠️ The Cauldron, which learned to want is too strong: the party is beaten down over 2 rounds. 🩸 Paracelsus takes the worst of it: 9 of the party's 57. 🪞 A tall silvered mirror shows the ethereal where it truly stands: weapons do full damage. 🔺 The party drives in as a wedge, everything committed forward. 💚 Mending Word closes John Dee's wounds mid-fight: 9 healed in round 1, then 5 a round while it holds.

🌑 Another march by touch alone. Walls, edges, and things underfoot take 2 from each of them.
