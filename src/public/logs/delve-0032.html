The transcript

⚗️ Room 1 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or dissolve the lock — the Cunning had already worked out the odds. The party chose to understand the mechanism and fix it.

🔧 The mechanism is understood and restored — it was a door-holder, and it shows the party how the builders meant to stand in this passage. The party takes that shape and keeps it. +22 score, and a shield wall into the next fight.

⚠️ Room 2 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to push through and take the hit, or search for a way around — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to bypass it with a utility spell.

The party files through and leaves the room behind.

📚 Room 3 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or study the shelves — the Cunning had already worked out the odds. The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🪜 Room 4 — stairs

Steps down, worn in the middle by traffic that stopped a long time ago.

There was only one option: the party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕳️ Isabella Cortese finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

🌋 Room 5 — disaster

A shelf of jars breaks over the party; what spills is corrosive and moving. The party must brace together or scatter.

They might have chosen to brace together, or scatter and regroup — the Cunning looked for the way that costs least. The party chose to sift the rubble.

🪨 The party sifts a fall of rubble: 25 gold, 2 materials.

🌿 Room 6 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to gather the materials, or move on — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to harvest the spout.

🗿 The alchemist bottles what drips from a gargoyle spout, dripping: 3 materials.

🕯️ Room 7 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to diagnose and treat the fever, or apply a natural remedy — the Cunning had already worked out the odds. The party chose to push through and take the hit.

🤒 Isabella Cortese is carried rather than treated, and the fever takes its price in full: 6 damage, and the party files through the next passage strung out around the litter.

🕯️ The wick is well down the oil. 2 more marches of light, then none.

✚ Isabella Cortese takes a wound that will not close down here. Healing can bring them back to 11, no further, until town.

👹 Room 8 — monster

An imp still angry about the jar holds the room (attack 11, health 22). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to speak with whatever wears the armour, or name the armour by its heraldry — the Cunning looked for the way that costs least. The party chose to pry the sarcophagus open.

⚰️ The party pries the lid off a stone sarcophagus: 55 gold.

🕯️ The wick is well down the oil. 1 more march of light, then none. 🚶 The party looks into the barracks wing (bunkrooms and a picked-over weapon rack) and keeps to the main route.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Cauldron, which learned to want waits at its center (attack 28, health 78); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 Chain Lightning opens the fight, but the monster resists the element: spell damage ×0.5. ⚔️ The party kills the Cauldron, which learned to want in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. ✨ Nothing is held back for later: the party looses everything it has, 7 workings in the one fight that matters. 🪞 A tall silvered mirror shivers, flashes and comes apart: 2 damage in flying silver. 🎒 7 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🕯️ The flame stands up, thins, and is gone. The party is out of oil.
