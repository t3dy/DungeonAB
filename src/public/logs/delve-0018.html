🕯️ Room 1 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to strip the gold leaf, or move on — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "Tithes flow both directions, technically." The party chose to rest and heal.

🕯️ The party rests at the shrine: 5 health healed per member.

🌋 Room 2 — disaster

The dungeon itself turns hostile. The party must brace together or scatter. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to scatter and regroup, or sift the rubble — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "Even the dead tithe here. Especially the dead." The party chose to brace together.

🌋 The party braces together and rides it out: 5 damage taken. 💚 Balm of Hours heals 14 as the dust settles.

🛡️ Paracelsus walks in front, which is where the blows land first.

💰 Room 3 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or search the room methodically — Simon Forman made the case: "The god counts. So do I." The party chose to leave it alone.

🚶 The party leaves the treasure untouched and moves on.

🪐 Room 4 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or understand the mechanism and fix it — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Fundamentals of Sorcery, volume three, page ninety: this exact mistake." The party chose to dissolve the lock.

⚗️ The lock dissolves. The mechanism doesn't open the door, but it doesn't need to now. +15 score. 🎓 It is answered from three directions at once and stays answered: +25 renown.

📚 Room 5 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells.

They might have chosen to study the shelves, or move on — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I know a spell for this. It's called leaving." The party chose to read the sealed texts.

🔏 The wizard reads the sealed texts: 2 spells learned, including a forbidden working. +50 score. 📖 The Grimoire of Low Whispers adds +1.5 to the reading roll.

🕳️ Paracelsus finds a hidden door into the flooded wing. Behind it: a floor that slopes down into standing water. Its rooms join the route.

They might have chosen to push through and take the hit, or search for a way around — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "The liturgy has a verse for this. Several, actually." The party chose to sift the rubble.

A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.
