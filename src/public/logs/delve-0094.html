The transcript

📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to study the shelves, or move on — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Overchannel? I call it generous casting." The party chose to read the sealed texts.

🔏 The wizard reads the sealed texts: 2 spells learned, including a forbidden working. +50 score.

🌋 Room 2 — disaster

The dungeon itself turns hostile. The party must brace together or scatter. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to attune the resonances, or endure the discord — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to brace together.

🌋 The party braces together and rides it out: 5 damage taken. 💚 Purify the Font heals 12 as the dust settles.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a hidden door into the burial wing. Behind it: burial niches, most of them open. Its rooms join the route.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A gelatinous horror holds the room (attack 6, health 21). The party must decide how to get past it. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to put it onto the floor spikes.

🔻 The party drives the monster back onto a bed of rusted floor spikes: 12 damage, and it has to pull itself off them. ⚔️ The party kills a gelatinous horror in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 💢 Sunder reminds the plate it was ore: it stops turning blows for the rest of the fight. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 4 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

💎 Room 4 — vault

A hidden vault, stacked with treasure. Vaults always hold something beyond coin. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to inspect it first, or leave it alone — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to loot the treasure.

💰 The party loots the chest: 85 gold. 📜 Also in the hoard: a scroll of Purify the Font, added to the grimoire.

🌿 Room 5 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to move on, or harvest the spout — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 2 bundles of alchemy materials.

🕯️ Room 6 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — the Reckless did not wait for a vote. The party chose to move on.

The party files through and leaves the room behind.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

🪜 Room 7 — stairs

Steps down, worn in the middle by traffic that stopped a long time ago.

They might have chosen to rope down the shaft beside it — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

👹 Room 8 — monster

A goblin toll-gang holds the room (attack 7, health 17). The party must decide how to get past it. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Robert Fludd made the case: "I disarm faster when it's already ticking." The party chose to sneak past.

🗡️ The rogue leads the party past a goblin toll-gang unseen. No damage taken; +15 score. 💡 Dancing Light revealed the watcher's position: +1 to the sneak roll.

⚠️ Room 9 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A tripwire runs up the wall to a bell: an alarm trap. Little damage, but the next monster will be warned (+2 attack). A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to solve the progression, or ask which square is safe — Robert Fludd made the case: "I've studied every trap in the codex. This one's new. Wonderful." The party chose to disarm the trap.

🗝️ The rogue disarms the trap. No damage taken; +20 score.

💰 Room 10 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to appraise the three chests, or take the heaviest chest — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to loot the treasure.

💰 The party loots the chest: 44 gold.

🕯️ The lantern is burning low: oil for 2 more marches.

🐉 Room 11 — boss

The boss chamber. The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault waits at its center (attack 17, health 48); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5).

They might have chosen to fall back, or open with a combat spell — the Reckless did not wait for a vote. The party chose to stand and fight.

🪄 John Napier uses the Haunted Armor — The Ghost Objects: a summon adding 1 attack each round. ⚔️ The party kills the Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault in 3 rounds, taking 31 damage. 🩸 It is John Napier standing in front of it: 15 of the 31 the party takes. 💚 Purify the Font closes John Napier's wounds mid-fight: 12 healed in round 1, then 6 a round while it holds. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 6 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🕯️ The wick is well down the oil. 1 more march of light, then none.
