The transcript

📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to study the shelves, or move on — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Overchannel? I call it generous casting." The party chose to read the sealed texts.

🔏 The wizard reads the sealed texts: 2 spells learned, including a forbidden working. +50 score.

🌋 Room 2 — disaster

The dungeon itself turns hostile. The party must brace together or scatter. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to attune the resonances, or link the three frequencies — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to endure the discord.

🔊 The discord tears at the ears and nerves, and the party comes out of it well apart, each of them having walked away from the sound in a different direction. 3 damage, and no line to speak of.

🛡️ John Napier takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind.

✚ That one leaves a mark on John Napier. Their ceiling drops to 13 for the rest of the delve. The Reckless do not stop to bind anything, and more of it stays with them.

🌿 Room 3 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to gather the materials, or move on — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to harvest the spout.

🗿 The alchemist bottles what drips from a gargoyle spout, dripping: 3 materials.

🕯️ Room 4 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to move on.

There is nothing to fight and nothing to take. The party keeps going.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

🪜 Room 5 — stairs

The floor ends at a stairwell. Whatever is below has been waiting longer.

They might have chosen to take the stair down, or camp at the stairhead first — the Reckless did not wait for a vote. The party chose to rope down the shaft beside it.

🪜 The party ropes down the shaft beside the stair and lands on the second floor. 🪢 The line goes down the shaft beside the stair: the party descends without burning a march of oil.

👹 Room 6 — monster

A goblin toll-gang holds the room (attack 7, health 17). The party must decide how to get past it. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to put it down the crack in the floor.

🌑 The party works the monster to the edge and puts it into a crack across the floor: 16 damage on the way down. ⚔️ The party kills a goblin toll-gang in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🌐 There is room enough in the chamber to fight spread out, well apart. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 1 more preparation holds besides — the ledger keeps it.

⚠️ Room 7 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A tripwire runs up the wall to a bell: an alarm trap. Little damage, but the next monster will be warned (+2 attack). A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to solve the progression, or read the dust — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "The safety margin is where the good magic lives." The party chose to bypass it with a utility spell.

The party files through and leaves the room behind.

💰 Room 8 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to appraise the three chests, or pick the chest that was opened before — the Reckless did not wait for a vote. The party chose to read the maker's mark.

🏺 The mark of Maestro Cellini seals the chest — his work alone was worth the journey. +35 gold, +20 score. 🎓 Done by people who do something near it: 10 renown less than a clean job.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault waits at its center (attack 17, health 48); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5).

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "There's a cheaper way to do this. There always is." The party chose to open with a combat spell.
