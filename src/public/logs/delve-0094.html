The transcript

📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to study the shelves, or move on — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Overchannel? I call it generous casting." The party chose to read the sealed texts.

🔏 The wizard reads the sealed texts: 2 spells learned, including a forbidden working. +50 score.

🌋 Room 2 — disaster

The dungeon itself turns hostile. The party must brace together or scatter. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to attune the resonances, or endure the discord — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to brace together.

🌋 The party braces together and rides it out: 5 damage taken. 💚 Purify the Font heals 12 as the dust settles.

🛡️ John Napier takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind. 🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a hidden door into the burial wing. Behind it: burial niches, most of them open. Its rooms join the route.

👹 Room 3 — monster

A gelatinous horror holds the room (attack 6, health 21). The party must decide how to get past it. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to fall back, or sneak past — John Napier made the case: "Plan? I'm the plan." The party chose to stand and fight.

🪄 John Napier uses the Haunted Armor — The Ghost Objects: a summon adding 1 attack each round. ⚔️ The party kills a gelatinous horror in 2 rounds, taking 1 damage. 💢 Sunder reminds the plate it was ore: it stops turning blows for the rest of the fight. 🛡️ Shields lock along the front rank and the party stops trying to win quickly. 🎒 3 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

💎 Room 4 — vault

A hidden vault, stacked with treasure. Vaults always hold something beyond coin. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to loot the treasure, or inspect it first — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to pry the sarcophagus open.

⚰️ The party pries the lid off a stone sarcophagus: 20 gold. ⚰️ The occupant objects: 4 damage before it is put back down.

✚ That one leaves a mark on John Napier. Their ceiling drops to 13 for the rest of the delve. The Reckless do not stop to bind anything, and more of it stays with them.

🌿 Room 5 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to move on, or harvest the spout — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 2 bundles of alchemy materials.

🕯️ Room 6 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to move on.

The party files through and leaves the room behind.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 2 rooms ahead and taking 2 damage.

⚠️ Room 7 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A tripwire runs up the wall to a bell: an alarm trap. Little damage, but the next monster will be warned (+2 attack). A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to ask which square is safe, or cross in order — the Reckless did not wait for a vote. The party chose to solve the progression.

📐 Every third square, then every fifth: the safe path is a sequence, and once it is written down the rest of the mechanism is legible too. The party crosses dry-shod and reads the next snare off the same logic. +30 score. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed further down will open to what was learned here.

💰 Room 8 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to appraise the three chests, or take the heaviest chest — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to crack the crates open.

📦 The party cracks open stacked crates and barrels: 12 gold, 1 material.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault waits at its center (attack 17, health 48); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5).

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "There's a cheaper way to do this. There always is." The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 The caster opens with Frost Lance, chosen precisely for the monster's frost weakness: spell damage ×1.5. ⚔️ The party kills the Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault in 1 round, taking 0 damage. ✨ Nothing is held back for later: the party looses everything it has, 6 workings in the one fight that matters. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 4 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.
