The transcript

📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or study the shelves — Michael Maier made the case: "Everything in this room fits in my satchel if I believe." The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🌿 Room 2 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to move on, or crack the crates open — Michael Maier made the case: "Measure twice, pour once." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 1 bundle of alchemy materials.

⚗️ Room 3 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to work the lab bench, or move on — Robert Fludd made the case: "It isn't stealing if the owner's a skeleton." The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

🌿 Room 4 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or search the room methodically — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "The light goes first. I merely follow it, loudly." The party chose to divine what is hidden here.

🔮 Divine sense finds what mortal eyes miss: a handful of coin scattered in crevices. +8 gold, +10 score. 🎓 Somebody checks the work who is qualified to: +10 renown. 🗝️ The trick of the place is understood now. Somewhere below is a door that does not know that yet.

🪜 Room 5 — stairs

A stair cut into the rock, going down. Cold air comes up it.

There was only one option: the party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕯️ Room 6 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or move on — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to strip the gold leaf.

⛏️ The party strips 30 gold of leaf from the altar. The next disaster will hit harder for it.

🌿 Room 7 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to gather the materials, or move on — Robert Fludd made the case: "My fingers itch. That means gold, or a rash." The party chose to harvest the spout.

🗿 The alchemist bottles what drips from a gargoyle spout, dripping: 3 materials.

🪐 Room 8 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to dissolve the lock — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "Even the dead tithe here. Especially the dead." The party chose to take one of the side passages.

🛤️ The side passage goes round, and round, and eventually through. Two more marches of oil burned than the direct road would have cost. +8 score.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Cinder Wyrm coiled in its forge-nest waits at its center (attack 5, health 15); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice.

They might have chosen to fall back, or open with a combat spell — Michael Maier made the case: "Every explosion is a lesson. Class is in session." The party chose to stand and fight.

⚔️ The party kills the Cinder Wyrm coiled in its forge-nest in 2 rounds, taking 0 damage. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🗡️ The daggers land before the argument starts: nothing comes back in the first round. 🔨 The Blessed Mace sanctifies the room between swings: whatever was stirring in the stone settles. 🎒 4 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ Michael Maier walks in front, which is where the blows land first. 🕯️ Someone checks the reservoir and does not like the answer: oil for 1 more march.
