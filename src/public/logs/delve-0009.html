The transcript

📚 Room 1 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or study the shelves — the Bold wanted it settled here. The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🌿 Room 2 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to move on, or crack the crates open — Michael Maier made the case: "Add nothing until you know what it does. Then add plenty." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 1 bundle of alchemy materials.

⚗️ Room 3 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to work the lab bench, or move on — the Bold saw no reason to be careful about it. The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

🌿 Room 4 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to divine what is hidden here, or move on — Michael Maier made the case: "Gold in, gold out — that's the whole science." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 2 bundles of alchemy materials.

🪜 Room 5 — stairs

Steps down, worn in the middle by traffic that stopped a long time ago.

There was only one option: the party chose to take the stair down.

🪜 The party goes down the stair to the second floor, burning a march of oil on the climb.

🕯️ Room 6 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to strip the gold leaf, or move on — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "Even the dead tithe here. Especially the dead." The party chose to rest and heal.

🕯️ The party rests at the shrine: 5 health healed per member.

🌿 Room 7 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to move on, or harvest the spout — Michael Maier made the case: "I've drunk worse than whatever that is." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 2 bundles of alchemy materials.

🪐 Room 8 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to take one of the side passages — the Bold voted to meet it head-on. The party chose to dissolve the lock.

⚗️ The lock dissolves. The mechanism doesn't open the door, but it doesn't need to now. +15 score.

🐉 Room 9 — boss

The boss chamber. The Cinder Wyrm coiled in its forge-nest waits at its center (attack 14, health 38); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Bold wanted it settled here. The party chose to pry the sarcophagus open.

⚰️ The party pries the lid off a stone sarcophagus: 20 gold. ⚰️ The occupant objects: 4 damage before it is put back down.

✚ Michael Maier takes a wound that will not close down here. Healing can bring them back to 10, no further, until town.
