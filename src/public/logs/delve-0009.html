🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or dissolve the lock — Michael Maier made the case: "I keep my hazards bottled, thank you." The party chose to take one of the side passages.

🛤️ The side passage goes round, and round, and eventually through. Two more marches of oil burned than the direct road would have cost. +8 score.

🕯️ Room 2 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to sing the third harmony, or attune the resonances — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Transmutation started as a hobby. It's a livelihood now." The party chose to link the three frequencies.

🔗 The three principles understand each other. Tension dissolves; the frequencies fade into silence, and the walls steady. +16 score. 🎓 Everything this asks for, the party happens to have brought: +25 renown.

⚠️ Room 3 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to disarm the trap, or push through and take the hit — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I did not survive the academy by standing in the open." The party chose to bypass it with a utility spell.

Nothing here needs doing. The party walks on.

🛡️ The order settles with Michael Maier at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.

🕯️ Room 4 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A silvered mirror leans against the wall, and it shows the room more honestly than the room does.

They might have chosen to bring the fever down, or apply a natural remedy — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "I'll scout it — from inside." The party chose to diagnose and treat the fever.

💊 The diagnosis is swift: a blood imbalance, easily corrected. Medical knowledge and a minute's treatment restore full vigor. +18 score. 🎓 Near enough to the discipline to try, not near enough to be paid for it: 10 renown short.

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

🪐 Room 7 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.
