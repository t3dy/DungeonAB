A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to read the place itself, or reconstruct his rounds — Simon Forman made the case: "The god counsels prudence. Loudly. Through me. Right now." The party chose to bless the font and drink.

⛲ The cleric says the words over a stone font of still water and the party drinks: 5 health healed.
