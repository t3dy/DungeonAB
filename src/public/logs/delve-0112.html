👹 Room 1 — monster

An imp still angry about the jar holds the room (attack 9, health 18). The party must decide how to get past it. It is a swarm: spell openings hit it ×1.5. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to put it down the crack in the floor.

🛡️ John Napier takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind. 🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 5 rooms ahead and taking 2 damage.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A pickled thing that finished pickling holds the room (attack 13, health 30). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to strip the insignia off it, or read its movement and walk around it — Robert Fludd made the case: "I disarm faster when it's already ticking." The party chose to sneak past.

🗡️ The rogue leads the party past a pickled thing that finished pickling unseen. No damage taken; +15 score. 👢 The Boots of the Quiet Step add +1.5 to the sneak roll. 💡 Dancing Light revealed the watcher's position: +1 to the sneak roll.

🗝️ A guardroom door with a drop-bar. The rogue has it open in the time it takes to say so, and quietly. 🚶 The party looks into the barracks wing (bunkrooms and a picked-over weapon rack) and keeps to the main route.

🌿 Room 3 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or divine what is hidden here — the Cunning had already worked out the odds. The party chose to search the room methodically.

🔎 Systematic searching reveals a cache in the oldest stones. Not as rich as it might have been, but real. +1 material, +12 gold, +14 score. 🎓 Done by people who do something near it: 10 renown less than a clean job.

The boss chamber. The Cauldron, which learned to want waits at its center (attack 28, health 78); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Cunning picked the safer angle. The party chose to shove it into the pit.
