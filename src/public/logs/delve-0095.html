They might have chosen to move on — Paracelsus made the case: "Run first. The reaction can finish without us." The party chose to gather the materials.

🗝️ Pico della Mirandola pockets the sexton's key. Somewhere below there is a door it belongs to.

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to link the three frequencies, or endure the discord — Paracelsus made the case: "Peer review can wait. The flask can't." The party chose to bless the font and drink.
