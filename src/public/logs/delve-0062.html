The transcript

🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to repair the gears, or divine the unstable motion — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Range is a suggestion. Watch." The party chose to compute the orrery’s epicycles.

📐 The fault is arithmetical, not mechanical: one wheel was cut to the wrong ratio and has been quietly wrong for a century. Worked out on the floor in chalk, corrected with a shim, and the spare bronze goes in the satchel. +1 material, +26 score. 🎓 Near enough to the discipline to try, not near enough to be paid for it: 10 renown short.

⚗️ Room 2 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or dissolve the lock — Cornelius Agrippa made the case: "A fight you skip counts double." The party chose to take one of the side passages.

🛤️ The side passage goes round, and round, and eventually through. Two more marches of oil burned than the direct road would have cost. +8 score.

🕯️ Room 3 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to bring the fever down, or diagnose and treat the fever — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "I asked permission for this spell. Twice." The party chose to rest and heal.

🕯️ The party rests at the shrine: 9 health healed per member.

🕳️ Margaret Cavendish finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 2 rooms ahead and taking 2 damage.

⚗️ Room 4 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

There was only one option: the party chose to move on.

The party crosses the room and takes the far door.

🌿 Room 5 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to gather the materials, or move on — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "There's a cheaper way to do this. There always is." The party chose to sift the rubble.

🪨 The party sifts a fall of rubble: 25 gold, 2 materials.

📚 Room 6 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to reconstruct the mosaic from memory, or imagine what the image should be — Cornelius Agrippa made the case: "The body is a temple. Mine's a fortress." The party chose to strip the shelves.

📚 The wizard strips sagging shelves: a scroll of Shelf-Found Working for the grimoire.

🐉 Room 7 — boss

The boss chamber. The Precipitate, everything the drains refused waits at its center (attack 13, health 36); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to open with a combat spell.

🔥 Cause Fear opens the fight, softening the monster before the first blow. ⚔️ The party kills the Precipitate, everything the drains refused in 2 rounds, taking 4 damage. 🩸 Most of that lands on Cornelius Agrippa — 4 of the 4 taken. 🛡️ Shields lock along the front rank and the party stops trying to win quickly. 🛡️ The party closes ranks: 2 less damage a round. 🎒 5 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ The order settles with Cornelius Agrippa at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.
