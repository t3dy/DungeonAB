A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to read the place itself, or question the ghost gently — Simon Forman made the case: "The god counsels prudence. Loudly. Through me. Right now." The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 2 spells.

🕯️ Room 2 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to link the three frequencies, or endure the discord — John Dee made the case: "The commentaries disagree. I don't." The party chose to attune the resonances.

🎶 The resonances snap into attunement. The chamber sings with one voice, the party heals 2 apiece, and they carry the chord out with them: the next fight comes 1 damage a round softer. +22 score. 🎓 Done by people who do something near it: 10 renown less than a clean job.
