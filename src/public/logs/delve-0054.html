🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

There was only one option: the party chose to break through the wall.

💥 The wall yields to force and the ceiling comes with it. 3 damage, a march of oil spent clearing the rubble, and what is left is a hole the party can only go through one at a time.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A ghoul between meals holds the room (attack 8, health 15). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — John Dee made the case: "Candles first. Then courage." The party chose to turn the undead.

✨ The cleric turns the undead: a ghoul between meals crumbles. +30 score. 🐍 A ghoul between meals drops a ghoul's paralytic gland: a weapon coating (+2 attack, venom), applied to Michael Maier's weapon.

🛡️ Paracelsus takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind. 🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

📚 Room 3 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to study the shelves — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to move on.

The party files through and leaves the room behind.
