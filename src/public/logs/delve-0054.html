The transcript

🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to imagine what the image should be — Paracelsus made the case: "The Work is a devotion. The explosions are incidental." The party chose to break through the wall.

💥 The wall yields to force and the ceiling comes with it. 3 damage, a march of oil spent clearing the rubble, and what is left is a hole the party can only go through one at a time.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A ghoul between meals holds the room (attack 6, health 11). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Michael Maier made the case: "As above, so below. Mind the fumes between." The party chose to drop the portcullis on it.

🚧 The winch lets go and a raised portcullis comes down across the monster: 6 damage. ⚔️ A ghoul between meals is finished before it can strike back. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🐍 The Blade of the Adder has taught its bearer what venom tastes like: the party shrugs this off. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 4 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ Paracelsus takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind. 🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party leaves it shut: the rooms it skips hold loot as well as danger.

📚 Room 3 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to study the shelves — John Dee made the case: "We are exactly where we are meant to be. Regrettably." The party chose to move on.

The party files through and leaves the room behind.

🌿 Room 4 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around.

They might have chosen to gather the materials, or move on — Robert Fludd made the case: "Traps are just puzzles with stakes." The party chose to crack the crates open.

📦 The party cracks open stacked crates and barrels: 12 gold, 1 material.

🪜 Room 5 — stairs

A stair cut into the rock, going down. Cold air comes up it.

They might have chosen to take the stair down — Paracelsus made the case: "Shake it and see." The party chose to rope down the shaft beside it.

🪜 The party ropes down the shaft beside the stair and lands on the second floor. 🪢 The line goes down the shaft beside the stair: the party descends without burning a march of oil.

⚗️ Room 6 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or understand the mechanism and fix it — Michael Maier made the case: "If it smokes, it works. If it screams, it works better." The party chose to dissolve the lock.

⚗️ The lock dissolves. The mechanism doesn't open the door, but it doesn't need to now. +15 score. 🎓 Three disciplines on one problem, none of them guessing: +25 renown. 🗝️ Whoever built this had habits, and the party has just learned one of them.

🕯️ Someone checks the reservoir and does not like the answer: oil for 2 more marches.

🪐 Room 7 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to repair the gears, or correct the orrery — John Dee made the case: "Tithes flow both directions, technically." The party chose to compute the orrery’s epicycles.

📐 The fault is arithmetical, not mechanical: one wheel was cut to the wrong ratio and has been quietly wrong for a century. Worked out on the floor in chalk, corrected with a shim, and the spare bronze goes in the satchel. +1 material, +26 score. 🎓 Somebody checks the work who is qualified to: +10 renown. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed below will open to this.

🕯️ The lantern is burning low: oil for 1 more march.

🕯️ Room 8 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

The party crosses the room and takes the far door.

🕯️ The lantern dies with the party still under the hill. No more light to carry.

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

🐉 Room 10 — boss

The boss chamber. The Shrouded King in his broken throne-niche waits at its center (attack 12, health 31); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. 🔻 The party drives the monster back onto a bed of rusted floor spikes: 23 damage, and it has to pull itself off them. ⚔️ The party kills the Shrouded King in his broken throne-niche in 2 rounds, taking 6 damage. 🩸 It is Paracelsus standing in front of it: 6 of the 6 the party takes. 📌 They do not let it climb straight back out: 4 more damage from the room. 💢 Sunder reminds the plate it was ore: it stops turning blows for the rest of the fight. 🎒 6 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.
