🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to imagine what the image should be — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to break through the wall.

💥 The wall yields to force and the ceiling comes with it. 3 damage, a march of oil spent clearing the rubble, and what is left is a hole the party can only go through one at a time.

👹 Room 2 — monster

A ghoul between meals holds the room (attack 8, health 15). The party must decide how to get past it. It is venomous: even a won fight leaves poison working, unless a cleric cures it. It will not face the cleric: undead take holy damage ×1.5. A boulder sits at the top of a slope, held by a wedge of rotten timber. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — the Reckless were already moving. The party chose to fight from behind the pillars.

🏛️ The party backs into a row of squat pillars and makes the monster come down one aisle at a time: 4 damage as it closes. ⚔️ The party kills a ghoul between meals in 1 round, taking 0 damage. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🛡️ The party closes ranks: 1 less damage a round. 🐍 The Blade of the Adder has taught its bearer what venom tastes like: the party shrugs this off. 🎒 6 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.

🛡️ Paracelsus takes the front of the march, and the rest fall in behind. 🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 3 rooms ahead and taking 2 damage.

⚗️ Room 3 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to experiment with the assembly, or understand the mechanism and fix it — the Reckless settled it by going first. The party chose to dissolve the lock.

⚗️ The lock dissolves. The mechanism doesn't open the door, but it doesn't need to now. +15 score. 🎓 Three disciplines on one problem, none of them guessing: +25 renown. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed below will open to this.
