The transcript

💰 Room 1 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to loot the treasure, or inspect it first — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "The safety margin is where the good magic lives." The party chose to open it with Knock.

🚪 Knock opens the lock at range: 39 gold taken. The noise carries through the dungeon.

📚 Room 2 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or study the shelves — John Napier made the case: "Plan? I'm the plan." The party chose to bless the font and drink.

⛲ The cleric says the words over a stone font of still water and the party drinks: 12 health healed.

🌿 Room 3 — materials

A room of herbs, salts, and quicksilver — alchemy materials, free to gather.

They might have chosen to move on — Michael Maier made the case: "If it smokes, it works. If it screams, it works better." The party chose to gather the materials.

🌿 The party gathers 1 bundle of alchemy materials.

⚗️ Room 4 — lab

An alchemy lab with a working bench. An alchemist with materials can brew or coat weapons here. An anvil sits under a dead forge, still true. A gargoyle spout drips something that is not water into a stained channel.

They might have chosen to work the lab bench, or move on — Michael Maier made the case: "I've drunk worse than whatever that is." The party chose to put an edge back on at the anvil.

🔨 The party works a cold anvil: anvil-set edge on John Napier's weapon (+3 attack).

🕳️ The floor gives way — a hidden trapdoor. The party lands on the second floor, 1 room past where they were, taking 4 damage, and the rooms between go unlooted.

🕯️ Room 5 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to diagnose and treat the fever, or apply a natural remedy — Margaret Cavendish made the case: "I did not memorize this spell to whisper it." The party chose to bring the fever down.

💚 The fever breaks under the working's touch. Margaret Cavendish is steady again. +15 score. 🎓 Everything this asks for, the party happens to have brought: +25 renown. 🗝️ Whoever built this had habits, and the party has just learned one of them.

🗝️ Margaret Cavendish pockets a sluice crank. Somewhere below there is a door it belongs to.

⚠️ Room 6 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. Scorch marks fan out from a seam in the floor: a fire trap. A frost spell can blunt it. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to solve the progression, or read the dust — John Napier made the case: "If it bleeds on me, that's how I know it's working." The party chose to spring it with a smoke bomb.

⚗️ The alchemist spends 1 material on a smoke concoction and springs the trap from a safe distance. No damage taken.

🛡️ John Napier walks in front, which is where the blows land first. 🗝️ A sluice gate, wound shut — and the party is carrying a sluice crank. The flooded wing opens. 🚶 The party looks into the flooded wing (a floor that slopes down into standing water) and keeps to the main route.

🪐 Room 7 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to sing the third harmony, or attune the resonances — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "The god forgives. That's the whole strategy." The party chose to endure the discord.

🔊 The discord tears at the ears and nerves, and the party comes out of it well apart, each of them having walked away from the sound in a different direction. 3 damage, and no line to speak of.

✚ John Napier is wounded again — 2 scars now, and nothing can heal them past 11 before town.

🐉 Room 8 — boss

The boss chamber. The Forge Tyrant, hammer still warm waits at its center (attack 6, health 14); killing it clears the dungeon. Plate and chitin cover it: the party's blows do 2 less damage each round. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Michael Maier made the case: "Every explosion is a lesson. Class is in session." The party chose to shove it into the pit.

🕳️ The party shoves the monster into an open pit: 11 damage, and it has to climb back out. ⚔️ The Forge Tyrant, hammer still warm is finished before it can strike back. 📐 The party meets it standing as the last room left them. 🔪 Six knives arrive before the party does: 4 damage before the first round. 🧱 The party fights from behind a row of squat pillars: 1 less damage per round. 💥 The Reckless finish the fight with style: +5 score. 🎒 3 more preparations hold besides — the ledger keeps them.
