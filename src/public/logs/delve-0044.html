The transcript

🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to push through and take the hit — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to bring the fever down.

💚 The fever breaks under the working's touch. Pico della Mirandola is steady again. +15 score. 🎓 Not done alone, and better for it: +10 renown. 🗝️ And the shape of the place gives itself away: something sealed below will open to this.

🕳️ Robert Fludd finds a trapdoor in the floor. The party ropes down the shaft and lands on the second floor, skipping 3 rooms ahead and taking 2 damage.

💰 Room 2 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to observe every detail, or divine what is hidden here — Robert Fludd made the case: "Doors, guards, promises — all pickable." The party chose to search the room methodically.

🔎 Systematic searching reveals a cache in the oldest stones. Not as rich as it might have been, but real. +1 material, +12 gold, +14 score. 🎓 Everything this asks for, the party happens to have brought: +25 renown. 🗝️ The trick of the place is understood now. Somewhere below is a door that does not know that yet.

🕯️ Room 3 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to sing the third harmony, or attune the resonances — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "The god helps those who check for tripwires." The party chose to move on.

The party crosses the room and takes the far door.

📚 Room 4 — library

A library. The party can study here to learn spells. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind. Shelves sag under books nobody has audited in a century.

They might have chosen to read the sealed texts, or study the shelves — Michael Sendivogius made the case: "A fight you skip counts double." The party chose to move on.

Nothing here needs doing. The party walks on.

🕳️ Nobody spots the door; the party works out where it has to be. The plan of the place demands a passage here, and here it is, into the burial wing. Behind it: burial niches, most of them open. Its rooms join the route.

🕯️ Room 5 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or move on — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "There's a cheaper way to do this. There always is." The party chose to strip the gold leaf.

⛏️ The party strips 30 gold of leaf from the altar. The next disaster will hit harder for it.

⚠️ Room 6 — trap

A trap blocks the corridor. The party must disarm it, avoid it, or take the hit. Half the ceiling is on the floor, in pieces worth stepping around. A bed of rusted spikes stands out of the floor, most of them still upright.

They might have chosen to disarm the trap, or push through and take the hit — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Why duel what you can outwit?" The party chose to bypass it with a utility spell.

The party crosses the room and takes the far door.

🛡️ The order settles with Michael Sendivogius at the head of it: whatever comes, comes to them.

🕯️ Room 7 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A font holds water that has been still a long time and is somehow clean. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong.

They might have chosen to rest and heal, or strip the gold leaf — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "Grace favors the well-prepared." The party chose to bless the font and drink.

⛲ The cleric says the words over a stone font of still water and the party drinks: 12 health healed.

🕯️ Someone checks the reservoir and does not like the answer: oil for 2 more marches.

💎 Room 8 — vault

A hidden vault, stacked with treasure. Vaults always hold something beyond coin. A portcullis hangs raised above the passage, on a chain that still turns.

They might have chosen to loot the treasure, or leave it alone — Michael Sendivogius made the case: "I hit hardest from the side nobody's watching." The party chose to inspect it first.

🔍 The party checks for mimics and curses first, then takes 160 gold safely. 🔍 The Masterwork Lockpicks found everything: the full gold taken, nothing missed. 🍀 Also in the hoard: a dead adventurer's belt buckle (+1 defense), now worn by Pico della Mirandola.

🕯️ The wick is well down the oil. 1 more march of light, then none.

🌋 Room 9 — disaster

The dungeon itself turns hostile. The party must brace together or scatter. A crack runs the width of the room, wide enough to matter and too wide to jump twice. A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible.

They might have chosen to pass a plan through the stone, or send something under the slab — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "There's a cheaper way to do this. There always is." The party chose to speak mind to mind.

📡 The link opens as easily as speech, and both halves know at once that the other is standing. They come back together at the far junction abreast and unpanicked. +26 score. 🎓 Done by people who do something near it: 10 renown less than a clean job.

🕯️ The flame stands up, thins, and is gone. The party is out of oil.

🐉 Room 10 — boss

The boss chamber. The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault waits at its center (attack 23, health 66); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5). A pit takes up a third of the floor. The bottom is not visible. Pillars break the room into aisles — something to fight behind.

They might have chosen to stand and fight, or fall back — Marsilio Ficino made the case: "The god helps those who check for tripwires." The party chose to shove it into the pit.
