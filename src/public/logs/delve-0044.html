🪐 Room 1 — situation

A connecting corridor. Nothing blocks the way; the party moves through.

They might have chosen to solve the progression, or ask which square is safe — Michael Sendivogius made the case: "I read a treatise on this maneuver. Chapter three. Brace." The party chose to read the dust.

👁️ Dust lies thick on the squares that never move. The party walks the dusty ones. +22 score. 🎓 It is answered from three directions at once and stays answered: +25 renown.

🕯️ Room 2 — shrine

A shrine. Resting here heals the party; the gold leaf on the altar could be stripped instead. A brazier burns in its bracket — nobody has been here to feed it, and it burns anyway.

They might have chosen to sing the third harmony, or attune the resonances — Pico della Mirandola made the case: "Fascinating. Everyone stand behind me while I annotate." The party chose to strip the gold leaf.

⛏️ The party strips 30 gold of leaf from the altar. The next disaster will hit harder for it.

💰 Room 3 — treasure

A treasure chest sits in the room. It may hold gold; it may be a mimic. A sarcophagus stands against the wall with its lid slightly wrong. Somebody stacked supplies here and never came back for them.

They might have chosen to appraise the three chests, or read the maker's mark — Johannes Trithemius made the case: "Traps are just puzzles with stakes." The party chose to take the heaviest chest.

💥 The heaviest chest was heavy because of what was packed around the lead. 5 damage, the party scattered across the floor, and only 10 gold in the wreckage — and it meets the next room spread out and unready.

✚ Michael Sendivogius is opened up badly enough that the delve will keep it: 11 is as whole as they get until town. The Reckless do not stop to bind anything, and more of it stays with them.

They might have chosen to read the place itself, or reconstruct his rounds — Isabella Cortese made the case: "Shake it and see." The party chose to study the shelves.

📚 The party studies the shelves and learns 2 spells.

The boss chamber. The Dragon Whelp of the Deep Vault waits at its center (attack 24, health 69); killing it clears the dungeon. It flinches from the cold: weak to frost (frost damage ×1.5).
