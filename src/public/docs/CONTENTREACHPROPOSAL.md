# CONTENT REACH — PROPOSALS

Ranked options per entry in `CONTENTREACH.md`. Recommendation ★.

The diagnostic question first, every time: **is it never offered
(generation), or never wanted (decision layer)?** They look identical and
have opposite fixes.

---

## For R1 — six mechanics under 5%, including `turn undead`

`turn-undead` is both problems at once: rarely offered, and taken 7% when
it is.

**★ Option 1: fix the offer side first, then re-measure.**
It presumably requires an undead monster and a cleric. Undead are
theme-concentrated (crypt, castle), so a party without a cleric in a
non-undead theme never sees it. Measure how often the *preconditions*
co-occur before touching the decision weight — the take rate may be fine
once the sample is not tiny. *Cost:* one measurement.

**Option 2: give it decision weight from party state.**
Same treatment as `BLUNT_ANSWERS`: a cleric facing undead should want
this. *Cost:* small. *Risk:* raising the take rate on an option nobody is
offered fixes nothing, which is why Option 1 comes first.

**Option 3: cut `cold camp` (0.2%).**
Below the census's own noise floor. Either give it a reason to fire or
delete it and its writing. *Cost:* small either way. *Risk:* deleting
content is irreversible in practice; prefer diagnosing once.

---

## For R2 — capability situations offered in single digits

Take rates are healthy (14–38%), so this is purely generation.

**★ Option 1: more riders, not more situation rooms.**
Riders cost zero room budget (`SIMULATIONFINDINGS.md` S4) and the eight
ridden room types are not saturated. Author rider-eligible encounters and
extend `RIDERS_BY_ROOM`. *Cost:* content. *Risk:* none.

**Option 2: let the true situations ride too.**
`astronomers-chamber` and friends currently only appear as situation
rooms. Giving them `rides` declarations would multiply their appearances
immediately, at the cost of their full three-beat framing.
*Cost:* trivial (a declaration each). *Risk:* a situation designed as a
whole room may read oddly attached to a treasure room — needs reading, not
just measuring.

---

## For R3 — `flee` offered 1600 times, taken 125

**★ Option 1: leave it and check the writing carries its weight.**
7.8% is above the 3% floor and fleeing *should* be rare. The real
question is whether it earns its menu slot in the deliberation beat — the
transcripts, not the census, answer that. *Cost:* part of the reading
pass in `DRAMATURGISSUESPROPOSAL` D1.

**Option 2: offer it conditionally.**
Only when the party is genuinely in trouble. *Risk:* removes the
*choice* not to flee, which is part of what makes a fight read as a
decision.

---

## For R5 — the census cannot see town, riders, or non-decision beats

**★ Option 1: extend the census to town encounters.**
Town reach is currently entirely unmeasured, and town has 39 options
across 8 encounters. *Cost:* moderate; needs a town-walking harness the
census does not have.

**Option 2: count riders separately from their host room.**
One-line change in what the census keys on. *Cost:* small.
**Best cost/benefit of the three.**

**Option 3: accept the dramaturg as the instrument for prose beats.**
It already sees them; no work needed beyond knowing which tool answers
which question (`MEASUREMENT.md` M1).
